How this builds on Shopify

Version 2. Everything in the prototype can be built inside the current Montone Shopify store with theme sections, metafields and a few custom Liquid blocks. Prices show as AED XX until Montone shares the price list.

One store, two ways to buy

  • The "For home / For your café" switch sets a cookie and swaps the price block, the collection cards and the product template.
  • Trade pricing: Shopify's built-in B2B (company accounts, price lists, quantity rules, net terms) where Montone's plan includes it; otherwise a wholesale pricing app with a trade customer tag.
  • Liquid switch on templates: {% if customer.b2b? %} or {% if customer.tags contains 'trade' %}.
  • Logged-out trade visitors see "Log in for trade price", never a home price that undercuts the trade list.

Product pages

  • Two templates: product.json (home) and product.trade.json (case tiers, spec sheet, cost per serve, sample request).
  • Metafields: montone.origin, shelf_life, case_pack, dose, spec_sheet (file), halal_cert (file), pairs_with (product list).
  • Sticky add-to-cart bar on mobile, gallery with lifestyle shots first where we have them.

A+ content space

  • Metaobject aplus_module: layout (banner, three-up, comparison, video), desktop image 1464×600, mobile image 600×750, heading, body, alt text.
  • Product metafield montone.aplus holds an ordered list of modules; one custom Liquid section loops and renders them on both templates.
  • The existing Amazon A+ images drop straight in. Upload a separate mobile crop so text on the artwork stays readable on phones.

Accounts and repeat buyers

  • New customer accounts with a one-time email code, so nobody has to remember a password.
  • Repeat view reads customer.orders for "Order again" and "Your usuals", and adds the whole last order with one cart permalink.
  • Trade accounts get a quick order pad, PO number on the cart, and pay on account once approved.

Pop-up

  • Shows once per 30 days to logged-out visitors only, after 6 seconds or 40% scroll, never on top of checkout.
  • Home path collects email for a first-order code (Shopify Forms or Klaviyo). Café path routes to the trade application.
  • The answer also tags the profile, so emails and Meta audiences split home and trade buyers from day one.

2027-ready checklist

  • VAT-inclusive prices for home buyers; trade prices excl. VAT with TRN on tax invoices.
  • WCAG 2.2 AA: 44px tap targets, visible focus, alt text on every A+ image.
  • Core Web Vitals: A+ images lazy-loaded as WebP/AVIF, no app scripts above the fold, INP under 200 ms.
  • Arabic RTL version, Apple Pay, Tabby/Tamara for home orders, PDPL-compliant cookie consent.
  • Product, Offer and FAQ structured data on every product page for Google and AI answers.

Brand strip, purpose and venue

  • Brand strip reads the product vendor field, so Montone, MilkLab, Mai Dubai and Rubicon each get an automatic collection.
  • Shop by purpose and shop by venue are smart collections built on tags (purpose:coffee, venue:cafe). One product can sit in several.
  • Tile images, names and starter kits live in a metaobject, so the team edits them without code.

Order planner

  • Custom section with a small script. Doses and pack sizes come from product metafields, so the maths updates when a product changes.
  • Formula per line: orders a day × share of orders × days open × dose ÷ pack size × weeks between deliveries + 10% buffer, rounded up to whole cases.
  • Logged in: "Add to order" fills the cart. Logged out: the plan plus WhatsApp number goes to the CRM as a lead with venue type and volume, which gives the sales team a ready-made quote.
  • Venue presets are starting assumptions. Montone's sales team should check them against real accounts before launch.

FMCG home page blocks

  • Offer ticker, brand strip, shop by purpose, bundles, seasonal feature, recipe rail linked to products, Instagram feed and trust row.
  • Bundles: Shopify Bundles app or fixed bundle products, so the set has its own price and stock.
  • Recipe cards use the existing blog posts (Virgin mojito, The Sky cocktail, The Red Cup, Raspberry mint) with a "products in this recipe" block.
  • Phone gets a bottom bar: Home, Shop, Recipes or Planner, Cart, Account.

QA log, round 2

  • FixedRecipe block made the phone page 782 px wider than the screen, so the whole page could scroll sideways.
  • FixedHero product shots were cropped at the edges on phone and desktop.
  • FixedCategory circles and gallery thumbnails could load blank inside the scrolling frame.
  • FixedProduct names, breadcrumbs and the desktop home/café switch were under the 44 px tap size for WCAG 2.2.
  • FixedProducts with no origin showed "Made in null"-style gaps; origin and shelf life now hide when unknown.
  • FixedCopy HTML copied the page with whatever pop-up or cart was open. It now copies the clean file.
  • FixedSearch icon did nothing. It now opens search with live results and popular searches.
  • OpenProduct photos on montone.ae mix cut-outs with lifestyle shots (coconut water, granola). The grid needs one consistent packshot per product.
  • OpenArabic RTL version is not in the prototype yet.
  • OpenPrices, case sizes, delivery days, minimum order and planner presets are placeholders.

Fix on montone.ae before launch

  • Every product shows AED 0.00 and "Sold out" — prices and inventory need loading.
  • An 18+ age check blocks every visitor on arrival; it isn't needed for syrups and creams and costs sessions.
  • Mango syrup description says "Raspberry syrup"; blackberry syrup uses the blueberry photo.
  • New products (peanut sauce, Mai Dubai water, chocolate callets, ube powder) have no description.

Placeholders to confirm with Montone

  • All prices (AED XX), case sizes (shown as 6), tier breaks and trade minimum order.
  • Delivery promise, free-delivery threshold and first-order discount code.
  • SKU codes shown are illustrative; we'll map them to Montone's real codes.
